Verdegree Studios

A quiet studio for software that helps people become.

Verdegree Studios LLC is a Colorado company. We make Relic, an iOS app for personal growth and reflection.

We build slowly, with care for privacy and for the inner life. Relic is meant to be a companion you keep on your own device, not a stage and not a feed.

Relic, the App of Becoming

Relic is a house of quiet rooms. Each chamber is themed on a planet and holds a different kind of practice: writing, walking, listening, or simply sitting with something. You can keep a journal, walk a pilgrimage, read optional sacred texts, and share a space with people you trust.

  • Chambers Rooms for reflection, journaling, and practice, each with its own tone and tools.
  • People you choose Couples, family, and community groups are joined in person with a QR code. There is no public social network.
  • Pilgrimage Outdoor walks and places across the United States, beginning in your home state. You may extend into other states with optional one-time in-app purchases.
  • The Scriptorium An optional library of religious texts in Jewish or Christian traditions, for reading at your own pace.

Relic does not use ads, and it does not require a Verdegree account. Your journals and recordings are kept on your device. Please read our Privacy Policy for the full picture, including a few optional features that can send limited information through Apple services.
